# Help Guide: Black Screen After You Sign In to Your Work Desktop

- Version: 1.0
- Date: 07/08/2026
- Status: Draft

If you sign in and only see a black screen, do not worry. Your files and work are safe. This is a display problem and does not delete your documents.

## What You Can Do
1. Wait 1 minute.
Sometimes your desktop appears after a short wait.

2. If the screen is still black, close the desktop window or sign out.
If you can see a sign-out button, use it.

3. Wait 2 minutes.
This gives the system time to move you to a working desktop.

4. Sign in again to your work desktop.
If it opens normally, continue your work.

5. If the screen is still black, restart your laptop or computer.
After restart, sign in again.

6. If it still does not work, stop trying more sign-ins.
Many repeated tries usually do not fix this faster.

## Before You Contact Support
Please have these details ready:
- Your name
- Your work email
- The time the problem started
- If the screen stayed black or opened later
- If this happened once or every time you tried
- If restarting your device changed anything
- Where you are working (office, home, or phone internet)
- A screen photo, if possible

## Contact the Service Desk If...
Contact the Service Desk if the screen is still black after two sign-in tries and one restart, or if you cannot get a normal desktop within 5 minutes.
