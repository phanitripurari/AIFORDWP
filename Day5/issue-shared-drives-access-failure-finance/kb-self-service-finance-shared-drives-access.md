# Help Guide: Cannot Open Finance Shared Drive

- Version: 1.0
- Date: 07/08/2026
- Status: Draft

If your Finance shared folder is missing or will not open, your files are not deleted. This is usually a sign-in setup issue and is often fixed with a quick reconnect.

## What You Can Do
1. Wait 1 minute after signing in.
Sometimes the shared drive appears after a short delay.

2. Close File Explorer and open it again.
Check if your Finance drive is visible.

3. Sign out of Windows, then sign in again.
This reloads your normal work setup.

4. If still missing, restart your computer.
After restart, sign in and check the Finance drive again.

5. If you are working from home, check your internet connection.
If connection is unstable, reconnect and try once more.

6. Stop after two sign-in attempts and one restart.
More repeated tries usually do not fix it faster.

## Before You Contact Support
Please prepare:
- Your name and work email
- Time the issue started
- If the drive is missing or shows an error when opened
- Whether sign out/sign in helped
- Whether restart helped
- Your location (office or home)
- A screenshot, if possible

## Contact Service Desk If...
Contact Service Desk if the Finance drive is still missing after two sign-ins and one restart, or if you cannot open Finance folders within 5 minutes.
